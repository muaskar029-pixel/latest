How to read an advisory

Each advisory has an ID, a severity rating, a status, and the affected and patched versions.

Severity ratings

Critical
Can be exploited remotely without authentication or user interaction, and leads to full control of a system or wide exposure of institutional data. Update immediately.
High
Leads to serious compromise of a system or its data, but needs a precondition such as local access, a signed-in user, or user interaction. Update as soon as possible.
Medium
Real but limited impact, or hard to exploit. Update during your next regular maintenance.
Low
Minimal impact, or a hardening improvement. Update when convenient.

Advisory IDs

Advisory IDs follow the format RSA-YYYY-NNN: the year the advisory was published and a three-digit number counting up within that year. For example, RSA-2026-001 is the first advisory of 2026.
